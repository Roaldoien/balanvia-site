Before contacting support

  • Confirm that you are using the latest version of Balanvia.
  • For CSV import issues, check that the file uses comma, semicolon, or tab-separated columns.
  • For missing reminders, confirm that notifications are enabled for Balanvia in System Settings.

Privacy

Balanvia stores financial data locally on your device. Please do not email financial records, transaction files, passwords, or other sensitive information when requesting support.
