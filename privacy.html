Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

In short: Balanvia keeps your financial planning data on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data collection

Balanvia does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party account systems.

Local data

Budgets, transactions, household members, savings goals, business settings, and payment reminders are stored locally in the app’s protected container on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. Version 1.0.0 does not synchronize this data through iCloud or a developer-operated server. The operating system manages removal of the app and its local data.

Imported files

Balanvia can import budget and transaction data from CSV files. A file is accessed only after you select it with the system file picker. Imported information is stored locally. Balanvia does not upload imported files.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Balanvia schedules local notifications for payment reminders. You can change notification permission in your device’s system settings.

Bank connections

Version 1.0.0 does not connect to banks or open-banking providers.

External links

Balanvia may open official tax-authority websites in your default browser. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If Balanvia’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the date at the top of this page will be revised.
